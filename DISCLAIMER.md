## Not medical advice

This repository is an independent educational study tool by a pre-medical student who is **not a licensed clinician**. Nothing here is:

- medical, clinical, pharmacological, or psychological advice
- a clinical protocol, guideline, order set, or decision aid
- a patient handout

All content is for MCAT preparation. Some explanations mention drugs, doses, disorders, and procedures to teach exam concepts, not to guide anyone's care. Nothing here should be used to diagnose, treat, dose, or manage any patient. Patients should consult their own licensed clinicians. **In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.**

## Study content and score estimates

- **Practice material.** Passages, CARS passages, data and reasoning sets (with their fictional studies and data), applied question variants, and full-length exam questions are original to this app. Recall questions are derived from the MCAT Milesdown Anki deck by MileDown and were converted to multiple choice and edited. The app contains no official AAMC exam questions.
- **Score estimates are not predictions.** Scaled scores, totals, and percentiles are study estimates, not predictions of an official MCAT score. The app's thresholds, timing rules, and score estimates have not been validated as a package.
- **Accuracy.** The question bank was reviewed against current references, including the AAMC content outline and DSM-5-TR, but errors are still possible. Check important facts against your textbooks or official AAMC materials.
